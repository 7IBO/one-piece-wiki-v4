The Final Saga is the eleventh saga of the manga and anime One Piece, and the fifth saga of the second half of the series.

In the aftermath of the Levely, the Raid on Onigashima, and the destruction of Lulusia Kingdom, chaos erupts across the world while a long-awaited global war begins to escalate. At Egghead, the Straw Hat Pirates get involved in a world-shaking incident and, with the Giant Warrior Pirates' help, flee to Elbaph, land of the Giants.
