One Piece Film: Red is the 15th One Piece movie, which was released on August 6, 2022. The film was first announced on November 21, 2021, in commemoration of Episode 1000's release. Eiichiro Oda served as a general producer and supervisor for the film.[2]

Synopsis[]

The story is set on the "Island of Music" Elegia, where Uta, the world's greatest diva, holds her first ever live concert and reveals herself to the public. The Straw Hats, pirates, Marines and fans from across the world gather to enjoy Uta's voice, which has been described as "otherworldly". However, the event begins with the shocking revelation that Uta is the daughter of Shanks.[3]

Plot[]

New Genesis: The World Meets Uta[]

Narration states that the world is in the midst of the Great Pirate Era, and while many pirates take to sea to chase their dreams, others care only for plundering and looting from innocent civilians. Thus, a great number of people worldwide suffer at the hands of pirates, and the Marines are not always available to help. Many people turn to the singer Uta to take away their problems, and Uta takes their requests seriously, promising to create a new age where they can all be happy.

A large crowd gathers on the island of Elegia to watch Uta perform a concert in person for the first time, as her previous performances had all come through Transmission Den Den Mushi. The Straw Hat Pirates are in attendance, and most of them are excited to see the world-famous diva. The concert soon begins as Uta opens with "New Genesis", and Luffy recognizes her once she throws off her jacket mid-song. Transmission Den Den Mushi broadcast the event to scores of people around the world, while those in Elegia are treated with a barrage of visual effects during the song. The audience cheers raucously once the song concludes, and Uta introduces herself to everyone. But inside a castle overlooking the venue from afar, an older man looks on and grimaces. As well, in the audience, the Jellyfish Pirates plot to kidnap Uta during the event to attain wealth and fame.

It is Luffy, however, who interrupts the concert first as he stretches his arm to swing onstage and reveal himself to Uta. After a brief moment of uncertainty, Uta recognizes Luffy and happily embraces him, leaving the crowd utterly shocked, including the Straw Hats. When Usopp asks how Luffy and Uta know each other, Luffy casually responds that she is Shanks' daughter. This sends the crowd into an uproar, and Uta can only smile sheepishly and shake her head. One person in the crowd says that Shanks attacked Elegia in the past, and Robin and Brook note that this adds context to Uta's decision to hold the concert here given the "legend" around it. The Jellyfish Pirates then come onstage to kidnap Uta, now intent on selling her to a big-name pirate to get to Shanks. However, Charlotte Oven then comes flying in to attack one of the crew before summoning Charlotte Brûlée through a mirror, as the duo wish to capture Uta for their mother. In the crowd, Helmeppo contemplates intervening, but Koby tells him to hold off.

The Straw Hats rise up to defend Uta, and Zoro, Sanji, and Brook overwhelm the Jellyfish Pirates, while Jinbe and Franky clash with Oven, and Luffy, Usopp, and Robin move to beat up the Big Mom Pirates' reinforcements summoned by Brûlée. Uta then interjects and calmly tells the pirates to stop fighting, saying it would be better for them to cease piracy and enjoy her music instead. The Jellyfish and Big Mom crews scoff at this, but Uta is unfazed by their threat and, with her attempt at peacemaking unsuccessful, says she will have to make a song out of them. As she starts singing "I'm Invincible", Uta generates a set of armor over her body along with a lance and shield out of thin air and becomes capable of flying unaided around the venue. She conjures a large variety of objects to barrage the enemy pirates with, and this culminates in her wrapping them in a casing resembling a music staff. The staff then unrolls in midair, where the pirates are left hanging completely stuck.

The crowd rejoices at Uta's victory, and Luffy is impressed with her strength. Uta announces to the crowd that while her video concerts tended to be short because she got tired, this concert would go on forever. However, she then turns serious as she warns any pirates, Marines, and World Government officials watching to not interfere with her concert, as there would be consequences for doing so. The Five Elders, watching the concert from Mary Geoise, assess the news of Uta's heritage as well as the threat level she possesses in terms of a potential uprising. Ethanbaron V. Nusjuro questions taking action against her, given she may be of Figarland blood, while Marcus Mars says that he is also concerned about the presence of Tot Musica; Shepherd Ju Peter affirms that they cannot be cautious given the threat it poses if awakened. At Marine Headquarters, Sakazuki informs Admiral Kizaru that he has been given the orders to subdue Uta on Elegia and warns that the island is the place where Tot Musica was sealed away in ancient times. Sakazuki orders as many warships as are ready to go, that being 30 in number, and the Marine fleet sets sail for Elegia with both Kizaru and Admiral Fujitora in command. On Whole Cake Island, Big Mom watches the events with several of her children, with Perospero noting that they had underestimated Uta's power. Big Mom expresses her desire to have Uta in her clutches at any cost, especially now that she knows her connection to Shanks. Katakuri steps in and volunteers to go to Elegia, wishing to be the one to save Brûlée from capture.

Back on Elegia, Uta takes a break in the performance to summon food and toys for the audience, saying she will give them anything they dream of. The Straw Hats prepare a feast using Uta's provided ingredients; as he peruses the food in his stock, Sanji notices a small pink and white mushroom and reacts with slight shock before disposing of it. Uta then appears among the crew and makes conversation with them before asking Luffy if he wants to play a game like they did as children. Luffy agrees, and Uta conjures a platform in midair with a plate of chicken for each of them to eat as part of a game of "chicken." She then conjures a charging bull on the opposite side of the platform, and the duo race to finish their meat before it arrives. However, Uta conjures a glass of juice and offers it to Luffy, which he accepts, allowing Uta to win the race and hop off the platform while Luffy is struck by the bull. Afterwards, Uta asks Luffy where Shanks is, but Luffy does not know. She asks why Luffy has his straw hat then, and Luffy explains that Shanks let him borrow it.

Nami asks how Luffy and Uta met, and Luffy responds that she came to Foosha Village with the Emperor and his crew. Twelve years ago, Luffy and Uta had a close bond as children; they competed in the same chicken game they had just done in the present, which Uta had likewise won by distracting Luffy with juice. They bickered as Uta flaunted her status as a full member of the Red Hair Pirates and Shanks' daughter, and afterwards Uta decided to sing a song for everyone, "Where the Wind Blows". However, her song was cut short as Luffy and the other Red Hair Pirates woke up from a sudden slumber and found Uta fast asleep. In the present, Luffy recalls how Uta disappeared from the crew suddenly, as the Red Hair Pirates had once come back from a voyage in solemn moods, not even acknowledging the young Luffy's greetings. As Shanks disembarked, he simply told Luffy that Uta had left to become a singer. Luffy asks why Uta had foregone piracy for singing, but she simply states that she wanted to be a singer more and has found great success in only two years.

Uta then asks what Luffy is doing now, and he responds that he is a pirate, just as he always wanted to be. This leaves Uta irked, and she bluntly suggests that he quit being a pirate and stay with her in this concert forever, leaving the other Straw Hats shocked. Luffy ignores Uta's proposition and heads to a rowboat to go back to the Thousand Sunny and take a nap, expressing his pleasure at reuniting with his childhood friend and her happiness with her new life. However, Uta declares that the Straw Hats will stay in her concert forever, and when Nami questions this, Uta sends her and Sanji onto a music staff.

With the support of the crowd, she turns against the Straw Hats and conjures flying soldiers to attack them; every time the Straw Hats attack these enemies, they dissipate into music notes before re-forming. Uta tells Luffy again to quit being a pirate if he wants to be her friend, but Luffy refuses while declining to fight her. Uta goes forward with her attack regardless as she sings "Backlight", and she quickly wraps the remaining Straw Hats in a music staff and binds Luffy with a green rope. She tells Luffy she cannot let him become the Pirate King and asks the audience how they feel about pirates, to which they respond with accounts of the horrible things pirates have inflicted on them. Uta frees Luffy of his bindings, but he is then doused in seawater and collapses as four Uta fans approach him with intent to harm. However, Bartolomeo arrives onstage and surrounds himself and Luffy with a barrier; the duo are then teleported away and replaced by a rock, leading Uta to conclude that there are more pirates in the area.

Fleeting Lullaby: Uta's Power Unfurled[]

Luffy and Bartolomeo are warped to a bridge spanning two towers next to the concert venue, and Law and Bepo approach them, Law having come to chaperone his Uta fan crewmate. Law warns Luffy that they must figure out Uta's power, and Luffy is unfazed, but Uta soon spots them and tells the crowd to assist her in capturing the pirates. Bringing out a massive moving contraption made out of an assortment of musical instruments, Uta rides it toward the pirates with all the concertgoers following her. Koby and Helmeppo join the crowd and try to contact SWORD via Den Den Mushi but cannot get a signal. Blueno then greets the duo, and Koby quickly prevents his companion from starting a jurisdictional argument with Cipher Pol, assuring Blueno that they know nothing more than he does. Asked if he can use his Doa Doa no Mi powers here, the CP0 agent affirms this but says it is too big a strain on his stamina to do it now. Koby decides to focus on recruiting more allies to help in the fight.

Bartolomeo carries Luffy while following Law and Bepo, and the group observes the large numbers of Uta's music soldiers flying around Elegia. However, the old man then calls out to them from a nearby building and takes them to a cathedral. The man introduces himself as Gordon, the former ruler of Elegia. Bartolomeo questions where the island's residents are, and Law reveals that in the past, Elegia was known as the capital of music. However, its citizens all disappeared one day in an attack attributed to a certain pirate. Gordon then explains Uta's past; following the loss of Elegia's citizens, he had raised her on his own. The two of them lived in complete isolation, and so Gordon trained Uta to be a great singer but did not know how he would introduce her to the world. Uta accomplished this on her own, however, after finding several Transmission Den Den Mushi washed ashore and using them to broadcast her songs. But while she found joy and purpose in her outreach across the world, Uta soon came to learn about the suffering many of her fans endured due to the Great Pirate Era. The fans started viewing Uta as a respite and even a savior, and Uta took their messages so seriously that she is now going forward with a plan to create a new era for their sakes.

Gordon pleads for Luffy to stop Uta's plan using their childhood friendship, but the conversation is then interrupted as Uta enters the cathedral and chibifies Bepo. Luffy asks Uta why she left Shanks' ship, but she angrily responds that she does not wish to talk about Shanks as she wraps Luffy in music notes and takes his straw hat. Uta once again invites Luffy to live happily in her concert forever, but he still refuses to consider it and demands his hat back. Law then manages to teleport Luffy, Bartolomeo, and himself out of the cathedral, and Uta tells her fans to make a game out of hunting down the pirates. She stays behind in the cathedral with Gordon, who reveals his knowledge of her plan and pleads for her not to go through with it. Uta responds by trapping her guardian on a music staff for the time being, and when Gordon brings up the threat of the World Government and Marines, Uta says she has a countermeasure as she unveils the sheet music for "Tot Musica" which she found beneath the castle; she questions why Gordon left it intact if he is supposedly against its use. Uta ignores Gordon's continued protests and flies off, leaving Gordon alone with Bepo.

Luffy, Law, and Bartolomeo remain on the move outside, with Law having Bartolomeo encase Luffy in a barrier ball and roll it to prevent the pirate from attempting to get his hat back from Uta. Law plots to head to the port in the hopes of finding Marines or government agents and getting them to share their potential knowledge of Uta's powers. While he is talking, he fails to notice that Bartolomeo is rolling his barrier ball straight off the cliff, and Bartolomeo and Luffy wind up bouncing around the island until landing on the port. Not seeing the Thousand Sunny anywhere, Luffy runs around panicking until discovering that the ship has been turned into a small anthropomorphic creature. Luffy also notes that he does not see any Marines approaching the island.

However, the Marine fleet arrives on Elegia right at that moment and commences its invasion, though the sky above them is cloudy and raining in contrast to the clear sky Luffy and the concertgoers have been under, and the sailors initially cannot find anyone on the island. Momonga heads to the concert venue and finds all of the concertgoers, including the Straw Hats, Law, Koby, and Helmeppo, all lying fast asleep in the spots they were watching the concert from. Uta then approaches Momonga, and as Marines, including Fujitora move in to surround her, she warns them that killing her will leave the concertgoers asleep forever. Holding a basket with Luffy's straw hat and several pink mushrooms, Uta takes one of them and eats it. Fujitora recognizes the scent as that of wake-shrooms, which are fungi that keep the consumer from sleeping at the cost of their life. The Marines realize in shock that Uta is intending on keeping the concertgoers in her dreamworld forever by killing herself, but Uta sees nothing wrong with this and disregards their attempts at reasoning with her as she begins to sing. The Marines immediately don noise-canceling headsets to render Uta's singing powerless but are then suddenly attacked by the sleeping Koby and Helmeppo. Uta commands all of the concertgoers to rise against the Marines, and they all get up and move to attack while still asleep. Fujitora orders his men to not harm the innocent civilians being used as pawns, but this leaves them powerless as the concertgoers start ripping off their headsets. Uta starts singing "Fleeting Lullaby", successfully sending more and more Marines into her dreamworld.

In the dreamworld, the Straw Hats try to deduce how they can free themselves from the music staff. Noting that Uta wanted to make them into a song, Robin wonders if their positions might correspond to musical notes. Koby, Helmeppo, and Blueno then emerge from an Air Door in front of them, and Koby confirms this deduction, telling Brook to sing a set of notes. As Brook hits each note, he frees himself and each of his crewmates in sequence. Oven asks for the group to at least free his sister, and Koby agrees on the condition that they agree to work with the Marines. In the cathedral, Gordon unsuccessfully attempts to sing his note before Bepo eventually hits it, freeing him. Afterwards, Koby and Blueno go to Luffy's group, and Koby offers to explain Uta's powers to them. He reveals that they are currently in an alternate dimension known as Uta World, which is manifested through the Uta Uta no Mi. Anyone who hears Uta sing will be transported into this space, whose reality is completely controlled by her. Luffy then realizes he had experienced this power during their childhood, as he had fallen asleep every time Uta sang. Koby and Blueno explain that maintaining Uta World drastically drains the user's stamina, and the world will cease to exist once the user falls asleep; however, they know Uta has been consuming wake-shrooms to be able to maintain Uta World. The constant consumption will soon lead to her death, and if Uta World is manifested at that point, everyone inside it will be cut off from reality forever; Blueno estimates that Uta has less than two hours left to live.

Koby's group is now working to band with the pirates to find a way out, and Blueno tells Luffy that his crew went to Elegia's castle to find answers. Robin is particularly interested in learning more about a certain incident from the island's past, saying it may give insight into Uta's power. After entering a set of underground tunnels, a group of rats points Chopper and the crew to a room full of books. The Straw Hats make their way to a massive library deep within the castle but are swiftly attacked by a group of massive stone golems that fire lasers from their eyes. The crew quickly goes on the counterattack, assuring Robin they will hold off the guards while she searches the room's contents. Robin quickly takes notice of a mural on the library's ceiling that contains ancient text.

Uta returns to the concert hall to resume her performance in Uta World, while in the real world she declares that her new era will soon be at hand. Kalifa reports to Rob Lucci from real-world Elegia that they only have about an hour left and that Uta's singing is also affecting those listening from Den Den Mushi. Lucci reports this to the Five Elders, estimating that up to 70 percent of the world's population will be trapped in Uta World, which the Elders remark would be an apocalyptic event. In Uta World, the concert is soon interrupted again as Saint Rosward Charlos comes onstage with his bodyguards, wishing to take Uta as a slave to sing to him. A group of off-duty Marines who had come to the concert quickly don their hats to assist the World Noble, and Uta notes she is aware of the Celestial Dragons after reading about them in a book. She casually rejects Charlos' wish, saying everyone will be equal in her world. The noble's bodyguards shoot at Uta for this insult, but she dissipates their bullets. Charlos shoots his bodyguards for their failure, but Uta, upset at murder being committed in her world, heals them. The Marines and guards move to seize Uta, but she traps them and the two guards on a staff before pummeling Charlos several times with a music note and trapping him as well.

The audience is left stunned by Uta's brazen attack on a Celestial Dragon, despite her attempts to assuage them that they are all equal in her world. One concertgoer, Yorueka, tells Uta that it is time for him to go home and take care of his sheep, but she does not understand why he would want to go back to a life with trouble and hardships. Koby and Blueno then come onstage via Air Door to tell Uta to return everyone to reality, and most of the audience recognizes Koby due to his fame from the Rocky Port Incident. Koby explains to everyone that they are not in reality and Uta has deceived them, but Uta brushes this off and tries to convince her fans how much better it would be to live in a world with only happiness and abundance. Some people agree, but the majority of the crowd comes to the consensus that they still want the comforts of their old lives and do not want to be in this diversion forever. Left bothered by a controversy she never anticipated and growing increasingly unhinged, Uta eventually snaps and tells the audience she will give them even more fun as she floods the venue with a bright-colored liquid. Once people become submerged in it, they are transformed into inanimate toys and food, and even Charlos and his guards are caught in it; Blueno and Koby narrowly escape through an Air Door. This alarms the Five Elders, who decide action must be taken to stop Uta even if a Celestial Dragon is caught in the crossfire.

Tot Musica: Teamup Across Dimensions[]

Koby and Blueno manage to reunite with Luffy's group, but Blueno has been chibified and is no longer able to use his ability. The Big Mom and Jellyfish crews arrive to band together, but Oven changes his mind upon seeing Luffy as he refuses to work with the Straw Hats. He and the other pirates then disappear into a mirror, saying they will figure out a way to stop Uta on their own, and Luffy and Koby's group has no choice but to wait for the remaining Straw Hats. Back in the library, Usopp sets the ceiling ablaze to allow Robin to decipher the runes on it. These runes read, "The fears and doubts of men...given the name Tot Musica. Now flee and cower." The rest of the crew continues battling the stone golem guards, but a rapid Radical Beam attack from Franky blows the ceiling apart and causes the castle to begin collapsing. Nami races to a mirror, knowing Brûlée is in there, and convinces her to rescue the Straw Hats by threatening her with the prospect of being trapped in Uta World and never seeing her family again.

Brûlée brings the Straw Hats and Oven back to Luffy and Koby's group, and Robin reveals that those trapped in Uta World are not able to get themselves back to reality no matter what. However, there is a chance if the fruit user summons Tot Musica, an ancient entity comprised of negative human emotions that has been called the "Demon King of Songs". Once summoned, this entity appears in both worlds and acts as a link between them; if it is attacked in both worlds simultaneously and defeated, the Uta World will cease to exist. The group prepares to move forward with this plan for lack of a better option, but they do not know who would be there to assist them in the real world. However, Gordon then arrives with Bepo and says he knows Shanks will be there. Luffy asks him if something happened between Uta and Shanks, but Gordon stays silent, causing Luffy to head off to Uta. With no other choice, the pirates and Marines decide to go after him and begin attacking Uta until they get reinforcements in the real world.

Luffy approaches Uta atop the liquid surface covering the concert venue and fends off the guards she throws at him. He then activates Gear 2 and moves to use Gomu Gomu no Jet Axe, but Uta knows he is not trying to attack her as his leg snaps back to his body without striking her. Luffy tells Uta that what she is doing is wrong, but Uta says the same to him and questions why he wants to become the Pirate King. Luffy responds that he wants to start a new era, and Uta throws him down before placing two guards with their lances at Luffy's neck, mirroring Gol D. Roger's pose during his execution. Uta says that the Great Pirate Era started with Roger's execution and will now end with Luffy's as she starts tearing the straw hat in two, and Luffy wonders why she hates pirates now after her time on the Red Hair Pirates. Uta responds that it is Shanks' fault, claiming that he and his crew abandoned her on Elegia. Twelve years ago, after she had performed a concert on this island, Gordon was captivated by her talent and offered her the chance to stay and study the musical arts at the kingdom's world-renowned program. Shanks told Uta he would allow her to go, saying her singing may just bring the world together and that he would reunite with her once she was world-famous. Uta refused, though, as she wanted to remain with her family, and Shanks seemingly acquiesced to this.

That night, however, Uta awoke to find Gordon standing over an island engulfed in flames and with its entire population struck dead. Gordon told her that the Red Hair Pirates had used her singing to get into Elegia and had now made off with its treasure. A horrified Uta ran to the port to try to reach her family but watched as they sailed off laughing with the treasure in tow, their backs facing her and the island while a Marine ship pursued them. Uta cursed Shanks for this, and soon news of the Red Hair Pirates' apparent devastation of Elegia spread across the world. Back in the present, Luffy declares his belief that this is untrue and says that Shanks will come to save Uta, but she refuses to believe him. In the real world, Uta kneels over Luffy's sleeping body and prepares to drive a knife into his chest. However, a hand reaches out to stop her, and Uta looks up to see Shanks and the Red Hair Pirates standing in front of her.

Uta initially starts crying at the sight of her father but then starts laughing and says she is glad to be able to settle her score with him before her new era starts as she summons the concertgoers to attack. In Uta World, Luffy can tell through Uta's expression that Shanks has appeared as Koby, Helmeppo, Blueno, and the pirates arrive and attack. Uta fends off several of their strikes, but Bartolomeo then encases her in a soundproof barrier ball, which causes all of her conjurings to dissipate. In the real world, the Red Hair Pirates allow the concertgoers to strike them without fighting back, but the Marines then shoot at the concertgoers under orders from Sakazuki to stop Uta at any cost. The killing of her fans leaves Uta in great distress, and she deliriously tries to heal one of them despite being in the real world. The Red Hair Pirates are appalled by this decision; when Kizaru arrives to lead the operation, Shanks moves to attack the admiral and tells him to stay out of this "family affair", but Fujitora arrives to assist his colleague. In Uta World, the pirates watch as Uta breaks down inside the barrier, and Sanji notes that wake-shrooms also cause their consumers to become aggressive and lose control of their emotions. Finally, Uta decides to use her trump card and summons the sheets for "Tot Musica" before beginning to sing it.

The song allows Uta to break through Bartolomeo's barrier, and in the real and Uta worlds, the Demon King Tot Musica begins to take form. Once initially manifested, it clashes evenly with Shanks and launches lasers at the Marine fleet while shrugging off an attempted onslaught in Uta World. The pirates and soldiers in Uta World attempt to barrage Tot Musica with their strongest attacks, but it absorbs them without flinching, and they are also forced to fend off an onslaught of Uta's conjured guards. The transformed concertgoers beg for Uta to stop, but their pleas go unheard. With the encouragement of his crew, Luffy heads atop Tot Musica to confront Uta, but she strikes him numerous times with bursts of red energy before summoning a lance and hurling it straight at him. However, Gordon throws himself in front of the lance and is impaled in Luffy's stead. Uta expresses no regret at attacking Luffy for his belief in Shanks, but Gordon reveals that what she knows is a lie. It was not the Red Hair Pirates who attacked Elegia twelve years ago, but rather Tot Musica.

That night, the residents of Elegia had requested Uta to perform a variety of songs into the night, and her singing caused the papers containing the demon's song to make their way to her from the library. Uta unknowingly began to sing it after spotting the papers nearby, which summoned Tot Musica and caused it to absorb her. The Red Hair Pirates attempted to stop it, and ultimately it was sealed again once Uta became exhausted and fell asleep, but not before the country was left in ruins. A Marine ship soon approached the island, and Shanks decided that he and his crew would take the blame for the incident, even from Uta as he felt it would be cruel for her to know the truth. He decided to leave her on Elegia with Gordon, as he did not want her talent to be confined to their crew while they lived as outlaws on the run, and asked Gordon to make her the greatest singer in the world. As they departed the island, the crew decided to smile and laugh for Uta's bright future but could barely hold back their tears.

Overjoyed at the revelation that Shanks is indeed a good guy, Luffy jumps back toward Uta, but she strikes him with a red energy burst to keep him at bay. Luffy then notices Uta has one of his drawings on her sleeve; he had given her a poor drawing of Shanks' straw hat when they were children as a symbol of their new age. Luffy says that Uta's plan is not the new age they aspired to, but she refuses to listen to him as Tot Musica enters a new form, causing it to absorb Elegia in both worlds. The sleeping concertgoers start running away, and the Marines move to keep shooting them, but Benn Beckman holds them at bay. As she is surrounded by Tot Musica, Uta hears negative voices around her saying this is all her fault. She brushes them off, though, confessing that she already knew what had happened that day, as a year after she began her singing career, she discovered a Den Den Mushi with a recording of what had transpired. However, she felt she could not go back on her new era plan out of loyalty to her fans, whose wishes to live in a peaceful world with her music she had taken incredibly seriously.

Now fully surrounded by Tot Musica, Sanji commissions Brûlée to get all of the concertgoers to safety in the Mirro-World, and Koby formulates a strategy for some of the pirates to aid in the rescue while others focus on attacking Tot Musica. Gordon apologizes to Uta for not introducing her to the world at the proper time and for not bringing himself to dispose of the Tot Musica music, but Usopp and Sanji assure him he is a good guy for making the effort to care for her. Ready to enter the fray, Luffy activates Gear 4: Boundman and starts attacking Tot Musica. Shanks attacks the same area of the entity in the real world but does it slightly later, and Katakuri then appears and tells the Emperor that the two worlds need to land their attacks in the same spot simultaneously. Katakuri can see into Uta World through Brûlée using Observation Haki, as can Shanks and Yasopp. Shanks and Luffy then unleash simultaneous strikes on some of Tot Musica's hands, causing them to shatter.

While trying to calm down and think of a plan, Usopp successfully attains a link to his father via Observation Haki, allowing each of them to see into the other world. The groups in each world congregate together, and Usopp and Yasopp call out the same directions to ensure each of Tot Musica's limbs is attacked simultaneously. Zoro, Koby, and Beckman shatter its right leg; Jinbe, Helmeppo, Lucky Roux, and Hongo shatter its left arm; Sanji, Oven, Katakuri, and Gab shatter its left leg; Chopper, Robin, Bonk Punch, and Monster strike the left flank of its torso; Nami (using Zeus), Brook, Rockstar, and Limejuice hit it in the center; finally, Usopp, Yasopp, Franky, and Building Snake shatter its right arm. Usopp and Yasopp then tell their captains to go for the remainder of Tot Musica's body, and Luffy, now in Snakeman form, parries Tot Musica's lasers alongside Shanks as the two of them ready their finishing moves. They each recall their memories with Uta, and Shanks remembers discovering her as an infant inside a treasure chest his crew had stolen from other pirates. Uta was crying, but Shanks was able to make her happy by singing an impromptu lullaby; he then recalled the Roger Pirates finding him in very similar circumstances and said that maybe this happened for a reason. Luffy and Shanks then unleash their finishing moves, with Luffy entering his Gear 5 transformation while an apparition of a wolf manifests around Gryphon. Their combined strike causes Tot Musica to dissipate completely, and Uta remarks that Shanks must have been lonely too.

The World's Continuation: Uta's Closing Number[]

In the real world, Elegia is left calm and intact, and Uta collapses against a rock as Shanks rushes to her. Hongo throws him a vial of medicine, which Shanks says will cause Uta to fall asleep and save her life. Uta confesses that she missed Shanks, but things remain unwell on Elegia, as those in Uta World have not been brought back due to their souls being swallowed by Tot Musica. Shanks demands Uta take the medicine, but she hits it onto the ground and says she must bring everyone back as the musician of the Red Hair Pirates. Shanks helps his weakened daughter get on the stage, and Monster places a Transmission Den Den Mushi in front of her so she can sing to everyone affected around the world. Uta starts singing "The World's Continuation" and successfully restores the souls of everyone in Uta World. Following the song, she meets with Luffy in the dreamworld and playfully taunts him for not hitting her like he did when they were children. Uta then notes that Luffy has grown taller than her as she conjures his straw hat, which is intact again. She puts it on his head, asking him to become a man who is even more worthy of it someday.

In the real world, Uta and Shanks kneel over Luffy's sleeping body as Yasopp confirms that everyone has been brought back. Uta nearly collapses, and Shanks has her rest on his side. Uta apologizes to Shanks for believing he would not come back for her and thanks him. The Marine forces then surround the Red Hair Pirates, wanting to arrest Uta for her actions. In response, Shanks declares that Uta is his daughter and anyone going after her should be ready to die as he unleashes his Supreme King Haki on the Marines. The blast knocks out nearly all of the Marines, including even a handful of vice admirals, and so Kizaru and Fujitora decide to stand down and retreat. Kalifa gazes on the concert venue one last time before walking away. Uta asks if her fans will be alright, and Shanks assures her they will, saying that a new era is approaching while looking at Luffy. Uta sings "Where the Wind Blows" again, and the Red Hair Pirates carry her off of Elegia as the concertgoers begin to awaken.

Luffy awakens on the Thousand Sunny as it sails away from Elegia, and Zoro points him to the Red Hair Pirates' ship in the distance. As Luffy gazes at it, he sees the crew circled around a coffin, with Uta not standing among them.[N 1] He then turns and looks forward into the horizon, as does Shanks. In the post-credits scene, Luffy sits on top of Sunny and tries to speak to it while reminiscing of Uta. Luffy and his crew continue their adventure as he proclaims his dream to become the Pirate King.

Trivia[]

  • This is currently the highest-grossing One Piece film at over ¥15 billion, having surpassed One Piece Film: Z's previous record of ¥6.87 billion in only 10 days.[77] It currently ranks as the 8th-highest grossing Japanese film of all time.
  • This was the first film since One Piece 3D: Straw Hat Chase to have its title revealed when it was first announced.
  • The director of this film, Gorō Taniguchi, previously directed the OVA One Piece - Defeat Him! The Pirate Ganzack, which came out in 1998—24 years before this movie was set to release, and over a year before the release of the first episode of the anime.
    • The OVA's titular antagonist, Ganzack, has a cameo appearance in this movie.
  • This is the first movie to feature Jinbe as a member of the Straw Hat Pirates.
  • Uta was created due to Oda being tired of drawing legendary old men (presumably referring to characters like Shiki, Z, Gild Tesoro, and Douglas Bullet who headlined four out of the last five movies) and wanting to develop a major female character instead.[78]
  • The events of the film, while not canon, would likely take place after the Wano Country Arc, given that Jinbe is part of and sailing with the Straw Hat Pirates, and the group demonstrates most of the abilities they revealed or acquired during that story arc. They have also previously defeated and are acquainted with Big Mom's children and their powers.[79]
    • However, this movie shows Big Mom as still being an Emperor and alive and well, despite being presumed dead, along with Kaidou, by the world after being defeated in Wano.
    • Additionally, a member of the audience refers to Luffy as the Fifth Emperor, instead of one of the Four, and Koby and Pudding have not yet been kidnapped by the Blackbeard Pirates.
  • This film is the first One Piece media to show the Transmission Den Den Mushi, which debuted in the manga in 2024, and contains the first reference to Shanks being part of the Figarland Family, which the manga confirmed in 2025.
    • Gear 5 makes its anime debut in this film; it would first appear in the main storyline a year later.
    • In addition, Robin's Gigante Fleur made its anime debut in this film, though with different colors; it only appeared in the main storyline a few months later.
  • This is the first One Piece film to be screened in IMAX Theaters.[80]
  • This is the first film to have an accompanying album, Uta no Uta: ONE PIECE FILM RED, sung by the main character releasing a couple of songs before the film's opening day.
  • The movie marks Blueno and Kalifa's first appearances in the story since CP9's Independent Report.
  • This is the first movie to feature the Five Elders, the Big Mom Pirates and the Red Hair Pirates.
  • Lisa Ortiz, the voice of Chopper from the 4Kids Entertainment dub of the series, makes her return to the series as the voice of Sunny-kun and several background roles.
  • Three special booklets were distributed at the cinema in Japan: Volume 4000000000, Volume 4／4, and Volume 4000000001.
  • One of the special rewards distributed in cinemas in Japan was an alternate cover of Volume 104, of which 500,000 were given. The cover depicted Shanks and young Uta and Luffy, and was drawn by Oda.[81]
