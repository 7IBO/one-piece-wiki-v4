The Fish-Man Island Saga (魚人島編, Gyojin-tō Hen?)[1] is the seventh saga of the manga and anime One Piece, and the first saga of the second half of the series.

Two years have passed since the Straw Hat Pirates were separated. They reunite at Sabaody Archipelago and prepare to set sail for the New World, but in order to enter there, they must first go through Fish-Man Island. There, the New Fish-Man Pirates and Flying Pirates are planning to conquer the island.

Story Arcs[]

  • Return to Sabaody Arc: (Manga: 2010) (Anime: 2011)
  • Fish-Man Island Arc: (Manga: 2010-2012) (Anime: 2011-2012)


Trivia[]

  • The anime adaptation was remastered, retouched and condensed into 21 episodes from the original 57, airing between November 3, 2024 and March 30, 2025.
  • This saga, along with the currently ongoing Final Saga, are the only sagas without filler arcs in the anime.
