The Thriller Bark Saga (スリラーバーク編, Surirā Bāku Hen?), also referred to as the Florian Triangle Saga, is the fifth saga of the manga and anime One Piece.

The Straw Hat Pirates travel to the ghost island of Thriller Bark, where their shadows are stolen by Gecko Moria. Now, they must race to recover them before sunrise.

Trivia[]

  • The Thriller Bark Saga is the shortest saga in both the manga and the anime yet, running through only 5 volumes and 48 chapters for the manga, and 58 episodes for the anime.
  • It is also the first saga to have only one canon story arc, followed by the Wano Country Saga, as well as the only one to take place in the first half on the manga series.
