The Elbaph Arc is the 33rd story arc of the manga and anime One Piece, the second story arc of the Final Saga, and the tenth story arc of the second half of the series.

The Straw Hat Pirates, joined by Lilith, Kuma, Bonney and the Giant Warrior Pirates, travel to Elbaph, where Robin reunites with Jaguar D. Saul and Luffy meets Elbaph's imprisoned and disgraced prince Loki. The history of Elbaph's previous king Harald and his ties to Rocks D. Xebec, including the truth of the God Valley Incident, are revealed. Meanwhile, as the world reels from Vegapunk's shocking announcement, the Red Hair Pirates, Cross Guild and the Blackbeard Pirates actively search for the One Piece while the Knights of God begin to mobilize, with their eyes set on annexing Elbaph for the World Government.

Summary[]

Hunters and Hunted: Misadventure in a Land of Gods[]

While sailing to Elbaph, Oimo and Kashii explained to the Straw Hat Pirates that they had reached Little Garden and found Dorry and Brogy dueling with their fists. Since this left them at a stalemate, the two giants had since put their duel on hold until they returned to Elbaph and got new weapons. The Straw Hats and giants frolicked together as they sailed, while Jewelry Bonney finally got to see the sea horizon with her father Kuma.

On Gartel Island, Bartolomeo was subdued by the Red Hair Pirates after burning their Jolly Roger a month prior. Hongo gave Bartolomeo a vial of poison and ordered him to have Luffy drink it. Bartolomeo refused and drank the poison himself, only to discover that it was harmless. The Barto Club was allowed to escape with their lives, and they made haste in setting sail. Shanks admitted appreciating seeing other pirates care for Luffy this much, but Yasopp then declared that the Barto Club still needed to be punished for their actions as he shot down the Going Luffy-senpai with a single bazooka blast. The Barto Club sank there, leaving their statuses unknown.

On Hachinosu, Blackbeard reprimanded Avalo Pizarro for allowing Koby and Gecko Moria to escape. However, he was pleased to hear Kuzan's report that they had captured Garp alive, and morale was high on the island overall as Blackbeard's followers anticipated their admiral's next move. Blackbeard contacted Laffitte, who reported from the Umit Shipping headquarters that the Revolutionary Army was successfully carrying out a blockade on all food being imported into Mary Geoise. Meanwhile, Catarina Devon and Van Augur arrived at Hachinosu with Caribou onboard.

Two days later, the Thousand Sunny and Great Eirik were separated, with Luffy, Zoro, Nami, Usopp, and Chopper being on the Sunny.[1] After a considerable search, the remaining Straw Hats eventually decided to call it off and wait for their crewmates in Elbaph. They then read the newspaper and learned that Dorry and Brogy's bounties had been reissued after a century, being 1,800,000,000 each now. Additionally, the reports on the Egghead Incident attributed Vegapunk's murder to Luffy. Robin took notice of a picture of Luffy in his Gear 5 form, in which he was holding up his left arm which bore an X mark. Brogy noted that the young warrior Hajrudin was now following Luffy; Hajrudin had intended to go to Egghead as well, but had to stay because of trouble on Elbaph. Said trouble came from the "Accursed Prince" Loki, whom the giants stated had killed his father King Harald in order to obtain a legendary Devil Fruit. All of Elbaph's warriors had been needed just to subdue and imprison him, and one giant pirate believed that the world would be brought to ruin the next time Loki was unleashed.[2] As they drew near to Elbaph, Brook cut Robin's hair, giving her the bangs she had back when she had met Saul as a child.[3]

Meanwhile, Nami awoke inside a fortress made of giant building blocks wearing a different outfit. She was soon accosted by multiple giant-sized animals, and saw Usopp in the jaws of a giant cat named Iscat. A giant porcupine attacked Nami, and she electrified it using Zeus. The porcupine fell onto Iscat and shocked it, causing it to free Usopp. However, Iscat was only angered by the attack, and it chased Nami and Usopp before hitting them out of the castle. Iscat followed the duo down and transformed into a lion, but was then struck down by simultaneous attacks from Luffy, Zoro, and Sanji, who had already caused a stir in the land after killing and eating the "Ear God".[4] Left at a loss as to where they were and how their clothes had been changed, the Straw Hats decided to search the area to find answers. They came across a human riding a grasshopper, who called the place the "Land of Gods". He revealed that the human residents all served giant animal deities, causing the Straw Hats to realize that they had attacked and killed some of these "gods" already. The primary ruler of the land was the "Sun God", but the man warned that no one had ever entered this deity's temple and come back alive.[5]

Naturally, Luffy immediately began running to the temple, but unexpectedly ran into a large wall of glass, shattering it. This allowed the Straw Hats to see that there was a giant-sized room outside of this land, where they reunited with Chopper. They came to realize that the Land of Gods was actually a diorama created by a giant, and were confronted by more giant-sized animals. Not long after, the Sun God entered this room to find the Straw Hats fighting his pets. The pirates ran back into the Land of Gods, where they encountered Iscat, and they used force to make the cat carry them to the other side as the Sun God pursued them.[6] In truth, the Sun God was Road of the New Giant Warrior Pirates, who had desired to make the Straw Hats his playthings after finding them incapacitated by the mists surrounding Elbaph. He told the crew that they could not escape from this room easily, and upon shattering the mirrors on the other side, Usopp found a wall of solid stone blocking the way. However, Luffy broke through the stone with Gomu Gomu no Kong Gun while Nami used Zeus to strike Road with a massive thunderbolt, allowing the Straw Hats to make it out with ease.[7]

The crew found themselves outside a castle atop a mountain range, with a large rope bridge connecting their location to a nearby tree which dwarfed the mountains. They hid as Gerd and Goldberg headed down to the castle to confront Road over his capture of the Straw Hats, then headed up the bridge. However, Luffy decided to drop down to the mainland below, promising to catch back up with everyone. Down on the ground, Luffy came across Loki chained to the tree, and asked who he was and where they were. Loki revealed that they were on Elbaph, and introduced himself as the Sun God who would bring the world to an end.[2] The tree he was chained to was none other than Treasure Tree Adam, and he had been bound by these Seastone chains in the Underworld the last six years. Loki requested that Luffy find the key to his chains, offering to eliminate a rival pirate crew of his choosing in return. Loki then attempted to intimidate Luffy by bringing out his giant beast companions, and a group of humans who had aligned with Loki against Elbaph appeared too. They wondered if they could rob Luffy, remarking that they had not been so fortunate against Loki's last visitor, Shanks. Luffy perked up upon hearing Shanks' name, and when he asked if Loki knew him, Loki decided to refer to Shanks as a coward. This immediately angered Luffy, who entered Gear 4 and threw a punch at Loki's head which the giant barely managed to dodge. Attempting to calm the situation, Loki claimed he was only joking, and Luffy asked why Shanks had visited here and where he was currently. Loki responded that he would not share this information for free.[3]

The Straw Hats on the bridge saw Road running toward them, followed by Gerd and Goldberg carrying the Sunny. Luffy pulled himself up to the two giants, and Gerd explained what Road had done. Goldberg asked Luffy what he had done in the Underworld, but Luffy could not say as Loki had made him swear to secrecy. Road reached the top of the bridge at the Sun World to recapture the Straw Hats, only to be pinned by a falling branch trap set by Zoro and Sanji. Gerd and Goldberg then arrived with Luffy and struck Road in the head for his transgression. They revealed to the Straw Hats that they were Hajrudin's friends, and told them to head straight through the forest until they found Island Clouds which would take them to the village. Down in the Underworld, Hajrudin and Stansen hunted a giant moose to serve as food for the Straw Hats' welcome. As the Great Eirik approached land, a young giant boy named Colon called out to them to ask if they would be stopping at Mato's pub on the coast. Upon learning that they were heading straight up to the Sun World, Mato gave Colon a basket with his mother and father's favorite foods. The Giant Warrior Pirates used a stone called a Painter to generate a rainbow, which they proceeded to sail straight up in order to dock directly at the Western Village; Hajrudin and Stansen followed behind. The Straw Hats all reunited as they converged on the village, and they were awed at the beauty of the Sun World. The explorer Louis Arnote had written about the richness of Elbaph, but his writings also contained a warning for visitors to not overstay their welcome on the island.[3][8]

Welcome to Giant Country: Old Friends and New Paths[]

The Great Eirik docked on the island clouds by the village, while Luffy's group made landfall on a ship called a Svarr that could sail through the air with island clouds attached to its oars. Most of the Straw Hats were eager to partake in the welcoming banquet immediately, but quickly shifted priorities once Robin reminded Luffy that she wanted to reunite with Saul. Gerd took the Straw Hats to the Warrior's Spring below the Owl Library where Saul worked. When they arrived, Ange rushed in and said that Saul had fallen and could not get up, but as she saw her old friend on the shore, Robin knew that he was just recreating the scene of their first meeting. Saul surveyed how Robin had grown, and revealed that the flames from the Buster Call had melted away the ice that had encased him, expressing regret at what had happened to Robin in the 22 years that followed. However, Robin only wanted to focus on the good of her survival, and she and Saul tearfully embraced while confessing how much they had missed each other.[9] Following this reunion, Luffy offered Saul his thanks for helping Robin, and Robin told Saul that she had indeed found true friends like he had once assured.

Lilith then introduced herself to Saul, and revealed to everyone that she intended to set up a laboratory space on Elbaph where she could bring out a perfect clone of Vegapunk that was successfully brought from Egghead. Ange showed the Straw Hats the Walrus School which Saul had founded, and began a tour while Saul took Robin, Chopper, Lilith, and Bonney to the library. Some of the giant children started chasing the Straw Hats while acting like traditional fearsome giants, but they were then stopped and chastised by other students who opposed the rough language being used. The Straw Hats were shocked to find that the children had no intention of being ferocious warriors like previous generations of Elbaph giants, and the biology teacher Ripley explained that this shift in culture had been enacted by King Harald, who valued trade and diplomacy over warmongering.[10] Luffy and Usopp then got into trouble for scaring the kids by roughhousing them too much, but Colon then arrived and showed willingness to fight with the pirates for fun, as he did not go to school and showed more of an inclination toward Elbaph's old ways. Ironically, he was Ripley's son, and he told the Straw Hats that his father was a human. Inside the library, Robin got to see all the books that had been salvaged from Ohara, which the owl librarian Biblo could turn giant-sized with the Iku Iku no Mi. Luffy's group swung by the library, and Robin and Chopper told them to go on ahead to the banquet. Franky decided to split off as well in order to examine the Adam Tree, and Gerd gave Robin, Franky, Brook, and Jinbe Elbaph-style clothing that she had designed herself.

The Straw Hats, sans Chopper, Robin, and Franky, made it back to the Western Village, and the giants offered a toast to their friends as the banquet commenced. The crew soon met the 408-year-old village elder Jarul, who had a giant sword lodged in his helmet.[11] Road was forced to apologize to the Straw Hats, but admitted that being the Sun God is something that all Elbaph residents dream of. This caused an argument to break out, as the giants did not agree on the Sun God's purpose. Jarul told the crew that one of Elbaph's ancient texts, called the Harley, spoke of the world being destroyed twice before in the past, with the Sun God Nika being closely tied to both instances. However, the inaccessibility of the text has resulted in interpretations differing about whether Nika saved the world from the destruction or if he was the one to cause it. Jarul noted that Loki could be considered to be trying to emulate this figure, and when Luffy asked about the prince, the giants universally condemned him, saying he had tormented Elbaph since his birth up to murdering his father Harald. Following this event, Loki began rampaging at sea before being caught by Shanks six years ago.[12]

Ripley accompanied Franky in his tour of the Adam Tree, and they came across a mural which Ripley revealed had been dated back to the Void Century. The contents of the mural aligned closely with the contents of the Harley, which Robin got the chance to read in the Owl Library. She read of three different "worlds" spanning the course of history, with Saul claiming that the Third World referred to the present day. Though open to interpretation, Robin's reading of the Third World told of the Sun God guiding the world to its end.[13]

Down in the Underworld, Loki conversed with a friend named Mosa via Den Den Mushi; they discussed a traumatic experience that Mosa had recently gone through. Meanwhile, two hooded members of the Knights of God, one male and one female, suddenly teleported into Aurust Castle.[10] The Knights confronted the two giants standing guard and demanded to know Loki's location. When the giants refused, the woman began firing projectiles at one of them, causing him to keel over in pain.[11] The duo ultimately acquired Loki's location and headed down to the Underworld, where they invited him to join the Knights. Loki refused, and his insolent attitude displeased the woman, Manmayer Gunko. Loki's wolf companion confronted the Knights, and Gunko used her Aro Aro no Mi ability to conjure an arrow to strangle it. The man asked if Loki would change his mind in exchange for his companion's life, but Loki again refused.[12]

Gunko began pummeling Loki with her arrows while dispatching more of his companions, but Loki's answer did not change. Finally, Gunko's companion Figarland Shamrock told her to stand down, saying they should pivot to their next goal: bringing the warriors of Elbaph under the World Government's command.[14] However, Loki then made note of Shamrock's resemblance to Shanks. Shamrock admitted that they were twin brothers and had been together in Mary Geoise once, but Shanks appeared to prefer the Lower World. Loki declared that he would take revenge on both Shamrock and his brother, drawing a swift response from the Knight. To not waste time, Shamrock had his sword Cerberus transform into a three-headed dog. Blades emerged from each of the dog's mouths, and the three heads jettisoned themselves toward Loki and gruesomely impaled him as Shamrock and Gunko flew off. Shamrock said they would need to target Elbaph's children first, and ordered Gunko to call an additional Knight to the island.[13]

Later, Zoro tracked Luffy to the outskirts of town, realizing his captain was looking to help Loki. Despite Zoro's misgivings, Luffy wanted the information about Shanks and assured that he would beat up Loki if he was lying. Nami and Road then joined the duo to help guide them to Aurust Castle in order for Nami to take treasure from it.[12] Upon reaching the castle, they found that someone had broken out of it, and inside they discovered the massive destruction left by Loki and Harald's battle 14 years ago. Road revealed that Hajrudin was also Harald's son and Loki's half-brother, but was not accepted as a royal because his mother was a foreigner. Although Elbaph traditionally looked down on foreign blood, Road came to admire Hajrudin because the latter accepted him despite being a social outcast, with Hajrudin's goal being to unite all of the giants.[14] Luffy's group then found the guards that Shamrock and Gunko had accosted, and they thought that it was Shanks who attacked them, though Luffy refused to believe this.[13]

The group's search took them to the throne room, which contained a magic circle on the floor that Luffy vaguely remembered seeing on Egghead. A voice then called out to Luffy, and the group saw a human man sitting on the throne. The man introduced himself as Ya-san, the husband of Ripley and father of Colon. Luffy admitted his goal of freeing Loki, and disregarded Ya's warnings about the prince's power. Ya told Road to push the throne, which revealed a hidden passageway to a treasure chamber where Loki's key was kept. However, Ya said Luffy would have to go through him to get the key, revealing himself to be Gol D. Roger's left-hand man Scopper Gaban; during this time, the other Straw Hats learned at the banquet that Gaban was residing on Elbaph.[15] Gaban showed his sharpness in combat as he outmaneuvered Luffy and struck him with the Haki-imbued key; he then pulled out his axes and unleashed a whirlwind attack which Zoro rose to meet. The clash tore apart one of the castle towers, and Gaban was impressed at Zoro's devotion. As Luffy entered Gear 5 to fight alongside Zoro, Gaban suddenly yielded and allowed the Straw Hats to take the key. He told them to come see him again if Elbaph was still intact following Loki's release.[16]

Fears Come to Life: The Knights of God Invade[]

Shamrock and Gunko flew up to the Sun World, where Gunko manifested an Abyss circle which summoned two Knights, Shepherd Sommers and Rimoshifu Killingham, from Mary Geoise. Shamrock questioned why both of them were summoned, but they revealed that he was being called back to the Holy Land due to the dire situation it was in. Sommers and Killingham contemplated how they would manage to kidnap the giant children without being able to send them through the Abyss, and Shamrock suggested making a game out of it.[16]

The Straw Hats and Road headed out on the Svarr, taking the injured guards with them. Nami returned to the village banquet, while Road pulled Gerd aside to tend to the guards. He struggled to hide his reason for being at the castle, and Gerd quickly noticed his nervousness and forced the truth out of him. Luffy and Zoro reached Loki in the Underworld and found him grievously wounded; Zoro proposed leaving one of his chains locked until they could talk to him. Right then, the New Giant Warrior Pirates and Sanji came crashing down on a Svarr, whose cloud support dissolved in the Underworld's altitude, as they were desperately attempting to prevent Loki's release. Luffy cushioned their landing with Gomu Gomu no Fusen, and Hajrudin pleaded for him to not trust Loki. However, Loki was then able to stand up, only one ankle still bound, and began to grab his hammer Ragnir.[17]

Loki threatened to bring Elbaph to ruin unless he was fully released, and swung Ragnir into the Adam Tree, summoning a lightning bolt which ignited fires in the Sun World.[18] Luffy moved to attack Loki in response, and the giant then collapsed as the pirates realized he had attempted to bluff his way to freedom. Luffy asked Hajrudin to save Loki's life, and Gerd gave him medical treatment. As he recalled Loki's horrible behavior to his mother in their youth and his killing of their father, Hajrudin quickly grew enraged and moved to kill his half-brother. Loki invited Hajrudin to give in to his petty anger but also implored his brother to consider whether he truly believed Loki had killed Harald with murder in his heart.[19]

At the same time, the Walrus School came under attack by a massive Jörmungandr, but Principal Kiba and Saul quickly beat it down. However, they discovered gym teacher Wolf laying bloodied on the ground, and Wolf warned them not to touch the children. Some children then reported that a group of children led by Ylva were starting to wander off while asleep. As the staff tried to figure out what was happening, they saw more monsters in the distance. Ange then went up to Kiba and Saul and revealed that she recognized these monsters, as they looked exactly like her students' drawings of their worst fears.[18] Saul raced to try and stop the children, and tried grabbing Ylva despite Wolf's warning; as a result, he was pierced by an invisible ring of thorns surrounding her. These thorns had been generated by Sommers' Iba Iba no Mi, and Killingham had put the children to sleep and manifested their nightmares into MMA with his Ryu Ryu no Mi, Model: Kirin, while Gunko used her arrows to lead the children down Branch Route 8 to an escape ship. While their powers were in action, the three Knights were able to relax and enjoy a meal. However, the children were headed into an inferno that Loki's lightning strike had caused, and a Fire-Fighting Svarr moving to put it out ended up being struck down by a MMA.[19]

Ripley took Franky to an ancient facility, which she revealed was over 3000 years old; Lilith and Bonney decided to set up their base of operations there. Right then, the group was attacked by a lightning MMA. The villagers raced to take on the MMA, and one adult prevented Colon from joining the fight. Nami informed Jarul that two human intruders had been spotted in Aurust Castle, and she, Usopp, Brook, and Jinbe decided to go look for them while the giants handled the beasts. They encountered Colon packing a Svarr, and he invited them to ride alongside him into battle as the Colon Pirates. Following her meal, Gunko put on headphones to listen to her favorite song, "New World". Saul attempted to use shields to stop the children from moving, but found that the thorns hurt the children if they bumped into each other. He then attempted to hold them back with rope, but a Fenrir MMA arrived to attack him, and Robin suggested searching for the source of these powers. The villagers began to be overwhelmed by a ghost and Nidhöggr MMA, but Dorry and Brogy then arrived on a Svarr alongside Gaban and took them down with Hakoku.[20]

The firefighters got into position to douse the flames, but could not reduce them as the children drew near to the fire. However, Jinbe was able to put out the blaze using Fish-Man Jujutsu, allowing the children to continue walking safely. The Colon Pirates' celebration was short-lived, though, as Gunko appeared aboard their Svarr.[21] Brook recognized her as one of the Knights of God, and Gunko proclaimed that they had come to subjugate Elbaph in anticipation of a Great War foretold by history that would end the world as they knew it; it was believed that the allegiance of the giants would play a critical role in the war's outcome. Usopp fired a Skull Explosion which blew apart Gunko's body above the waist, but she immediately regenerated from this without issue. Gunko used her arrows to throw off the Straw Hats' attacks and hit them off the Svarr, while strangling Colon with another arrow. At the same time, Killingham subdued Kiba and Ange while Sommers brutalized Saul with his thorns as Robin and Chopper hid in his hair. Sommers derided Elbaph's newfound focus on pacifism and education, and cursed Harald for thwarting an earlier effort to turn the giants into the World Government's mercenaries.[22]

Gunko tied up Nami, Usopp, Brook, and Jinbe on the ground near Saul and Sommers, and told Brook that she would take him as a slave, but Brook refused even if the alternative was death. Killingham connected the Knights' Den Den Mushi to a Dengo Mushi, allowing Sommers to broadcast himself across Elbaph as the Giant Warrior Pirates' Svarr was overwhelmed by MMA and crashed. Addressing Jarul, Sommers revealed the names of the ten child hostages, which now included Colon.[23] He ordered the giants to burn down the Walrus School and Owl Library, threatening to have each child walk off the branch one by one if they failed to decide within 30 seconds, starting with Ronja. Gaban began heading to the branch, along with Dorry, Brogy, and several other giants. Robin moved to attack Sommers, using her sprouted limbs to break his spine, but the immortal Knight quickly restored himself. Robin then sent a clone of herself to force Sommers off the edge in an attempt to put him out of range of the children, but Sommers impaled the clone and managed to stick around as he sprouted a vine to hold onto the branch. Robin moved to free her crewmates in Sommers' absence, but he quickly pulled himself back up and wrapped thorns around her. As Sommers' first ultimatum was about to expire, Ronja said she would go forward with her sacrifice to save the library and school, but Jarul then ordered for Ange to burn them down.

As Sommers prepared to attack Robin, his hand suddenly went flying off, and he was surprised when it did not immediately regenerate. Gaban then charged at Sommers, who was horrified to see the legendary pirate again as Gaban drew blood with a Supreme King Haki-imbued cut to the Knight's chest.[24] The Straw Hats were in awe to see Gaban's attacks being effective against a Knight, and Gaban easily bypassed Sommers' thorny vines as he decapitated the Knight at the shoulders. Gaban then ran off to save Colon, and Chopper emerged from Saul's hair to follow him. Ange obeyed Jarul's order to set fire to the school, while Killingham shot a flaming arrow at the library. Gunko brought Ronja back from the edge as Gaban arrived. However, Colon immediately called out to his father, causing Gunko to move to target him. Gaban urged his son to be strong while he moved to save the children and the library, but upon foreseeing that Gunko would inflict a fatal blow on him, he decided to surrender instead. Gunko then impaled Gaban with multiple arrows and sent him falling down into the Underworld, but as Colon called out to his father, she suddenly had memories come to her. She recalled calling out for her own father in the same way, as well as interacting with Brook in the distant past. She started heading toward Brook, but was then possessed by Imu. Declaring that Mary Geoise was a "sea of fire", Imu declared that they needed to hasten the takeover of Elbaph as Gunko's body gained demonic features.[25]

As the children's relatives drew near to them, Imu subdued them and the children with powerful Supreme King Haki before summoning weapons from the Abyss to cause further damage. Imu instructed Killingham to continue producing and reviving MMA, and tasked Sommers with leading the children to the escape ship. Imu turned attention to Dorry and Brogy, telling them to take the throne of Elbaph by killing Jarul. Brogy grabbed Gunko's body, but Imu summoned a shotgun and blasted off his arm, saying that it would be natural for him to receive great power and become a demon. Imu then invoked the Demon Covenant and trapped Brogy in an Abyss, before flipping him around in a technique called Domi Reversi. From this Abyss, Brogy emerged as a demonic and monstrous entity who was now fighting alongside Imu against the other giants. He was soon joined by Dorry, and the warriors who found themselves in between the two captains ended up being transformed as well, resulting in just about all of the Giant Warrior Pirates becoming bloodthirsty demons. They marched toward Jarul and the Western Village, flanked by an increasing number of MMA.[26]

Loki's Lament: The Accursed Prince and the Era of Rocks[]

In the Underworld, Zoro, Sanji, and Goldberg hunted down a Groggysaurus to eat. Loki told Hajrudin that it was the Knights of God who had inflicted his wounds. Luffy fed Loki some meat and soup and asked for his information on Shanks, but Loki refused to honor the deal because he had not yet been freed. He noted Luffy's attachment to Shanks and admitted that he once idolized a pirate in the same way, recalling how he tried to join the Rocks Pirates as a child.[21]

Chopper ended up following Gaban off the branch, and he used Guard Point to cushion their fall. The Straw Hats noticed the fall and went to check on it along with Hajrudin, Stansen, and Road. Loki then received a call from Mosa, who assured him they were fine but that they would likely not be able to call him for a while. This sent Loki into a panic as he desperately tried to get more information, but Mosa hung up. Luffy's group then returned with Gaban and Chopper in tow, with Gaban needing urgent medical attention. Chopper revealed to the group that some bad guys had taken children hostage, but the pirates immediately realized the threat once they sensed Imu's Haki unleashed.[27] Hajrudin immediately summoned his crew to the lift, leaving Gerd and Chopper to tend to Gaban and Loki. Gaban told Luffy to be mindful of how he used his Supreme King Haki and that the Knights of God were not immortal; this caused Loki to realize the power he possessed. Gaban noted that Zoro also possessed this Haki, which Zoro himself was not aware of. Loki derided Hajrudin for relying on the Straw Hats over him to save Elbaph, but Hajrudin responded that he could not trust his half-brother. Luffy offered to help the situation by inviting Loki to join his crew, which outraged the giant. However, Gaban then advised Hajrudin to free Loki, revealing that he was at Aurust Castle on the day of Harald's death and suggesting that Loki tell the full story. Hajrudin gave Loki three minutes to do so, and Loki was not thrilled to have to recall that "horrible day."

Fourteen years ago, Loki and Jarul had been summoned to the castle, where they found Harald being impaled by his men. Meanwhile, Shanks was speaking with Gaban at his house; when Gaban brought up Harald, Shanks revealed that the two of them had met in Mary Geoise and there was something he wished to discuss with the giant. On that day, Elbaph lost their great king Harald, but he was not always known as the wise ruler he would become. Having ascended to the throne at age 45 (15 in human years), over a century ago Harald was a cruel and violent young man who regularly preyed on human settlements.[28] One day, he attacked the Bunt Kingdom to rescue a giantess named Ida who was in a cage as part of a sideshow attraction. However, Ida slapped Harald for doing this, as she was happily employed by the humans running the attraction; she challenged his notions of giant supremacy and ordered him to take her back. As Ida began helping the Bunt residents restore their town, Harald's conscience was pricked, and he had his crew go back and help her. Following this, Ida accompanied Harald on his ship, and they got to know each other. Once he returned to Elbaph, Harald declared that he would embark on diplomatic missions to perform peaceful cultural exchanges. Ida guided him in these efforts, and 81 years ago, she gave birth to their son Hajrudin.

However, the kingdom elders forbade Harald from marrying Ida due to her foreign origin and instead presented him with a pureblooded woman from Brewers Village named Estrid. 18 years later, while Harald was away on his diplomatic missions, Estrid gave birth to their son Loki. Upon seeing him for the first time, she was repulsed by his snake-like eyes and proceeded to throw him down into the Underworld to kill him. However, the newborn Loki survived the fall and managed to climb back up to the Sun World.[29] Estrid fell ill from then on and commanded her servants to cover Loki's eyes and prevent him from coming near her. A series of hardships around this time plunged the country into chaos, and rumors began to spread that Loki's "curse" was responsible. However, the next time Harald returned to Elbaph, he brought along a convoy of aid secured through his diplomacy, which alleviated his kingdom's famine. He came to find that Estrid had just passed away and held Loki for the first time. Although Harald showed pride in his son, even as an infant, Loki had already forsaken love.

Harald worked to rebuild Elbaph and design new branch routes to better connect the villages, but his work meant that Loki was left in the care of Estrid's older brother, who regularly abused him. 56 years ago, Harald spoke with Jarul about an incident he was involved in at the Levely that year. Jarul warned him that enmity has existed between giants and the World Government throughout history and shared the story of a band of shipwrights called Galleila who had been captured and imprisoned centuries ago. Eight years later, Harald was immensely popular in his kingdom, while Loki was seen as a terror who attacked villages when his father was away. Hajrudin tried to stop him from attacking a village, only for Loki to overpower him as he derided his half-brother's illegitimate parentage. However, Loki's outbursts were only an attempt to cover the void of love in his heart, and he unsuccessfully attempted to kill himself by throwing himself into the Underworld.

Following his suicide attempt, Loki encountered the human pirate Rocks D. Xebec and his followers Edward Newgate, Buckingham Stussy, and Shiki.[30] Rocks had come looking for Harald, and when Loki revealed he was his son, Rocks instructed him to send out a message that he had been severely wounded. Loki refused to take orders from a human and moved to attack Rocks, causing the pirate to actually attack and injure him with a powerful sword slash. Rocks had first met Harald eight years prior at the Levely, when Rocks had killed a Marine Admiral before breaking into the Room of Flowers and encountering Imu. Rocks knew he could do nothing against Imu but promised to the ruler that he would one day return. He went back outside, where he found Harald being pursued by the Holy Land's forces after the king had attempted to infiltrate the council. Harald and Rocks clashed blades, but Rocks then instructed the giant to aim his next strike at the town in order to facilitate their escape. The two went their separate ways from there, with Rocks hoping to meet Harald again some day. In the following eight years, Rocks had amassed tremendous infamy for his terrorism against the World Government and used Davy Back Fights to recruit crew members. Once Harald returned to Elbaph to check on Loki, Rocks invited the king to join forces with him, revealing his plot to conquer the lawless island of Hachinosu, take command over the criminals there, and overthrow the World Government to become King of the World. Harald refused, but Loki was awestruck to hear of Rocks' goal and so gained a new dream of following after the pirate.

Four years later, Rocks and his crew successfully took control of Hachinosu.[31] On Elbaph, Loki regularly attempted to escape to sea, and when confronted by Harald and Ida for making trouble, he derided Elbaph's weakness and said he wanted to be free like Rocks. Rocks made repeated visits to Elbaph to recruit Harald to no avail, and on each visit Loki tried to join him but was likewise rebuffed. On Hachinosu, Rocks told his crew that he desired to have Harald in order to achieve his goals of acquiring a legendary Devil Fruit from Elbaph and recruiting the Galleila band. A young Kaidou questioned why Rocks did not simply use force to make Harald obey him, but Rocks would not entertain such a thought because Harald was his friend.[32] Although Rocks' crew followed him out of respect for his drive and power, they did not get along with each other, and members actively tried to sabotage one another during a raid on the International Philanthropy Federation. Still, Rocks managed to successfully guide them in battle against a Marine siege, and the exploits of the Rocks Pirates were so great that Rocks was undoubtedly the top pirate of this era.

During this time, the top crews at sea alongside Rocks' band were the Roger Pirates as well as the Kuja Pirates, led by Gloriosa. However, it was her first mate Shakuyaku who was the top idol of the sea, and though Gloriosa was in love with Roger, he and his crew only had eyes for Shakky. Eventually Shakky became the Kuja empress, and Roger regularly did battle with Garp during this time as well. 42 years ago, the world was left stunned as news broke that Shakky had left the Kuja Pirates. The residents of Hachinosu soon received an even bigger shock as Shakky came to the island, saying she wanted to hide out and open her own business. Enthralled by her beauty, the pirates wasted no time in cleaning up the island and constructing what would become Shakky's Rip-off Bar, and even Rocks himself was willing to attend to her every want. Two years later, Roger decided to go to the bar to try and win her, as he discovered she had left a love note for his first mate, Silvers Rayleigh, telling him to come and get her. At the bar, Shakky noted that Rocks was no longer as infatuated with her, with Buckin deducing that he had met someone and become a father. When Roger arrived, he and Rocks quickly got into a confrontation, but they were prohibited from fighting in the bar. Shakky affirmed that she was only interested in seeing Rayleigh, while Roger turned down the advances of Gloriosa, who had joined the Rocks Pirates to try and pursue him.[33]

Meanwhile, Harald came into conflict with the World Government when he defended the unaffiliated country Enishi from an assault by government agents. Despite this, he still aspired to have Elbaph affiliate with the World Government someday rather than align with Rocks. Finally, the Five Elders told Harald that Elbaph could gain affiliation, but only on the condition that he kill Rocks.[34] The next time Rocks came to Elbaph, Harald followed the Elders' instructions and attacked him, causing a big fight to break out. Rocks claimed that the World Government was only interested in Elbaph for its military power, but Harald responded that Elbaph's warmongering past was a shackle on his kingdom's potential to grow, and he believed joining the World Government was the only way to achieve true progress. The two men's friendship came to an end that day, and Rocks would not see Harald again until the time of the God Valley Incident.[33]

Island of Fate: The God Valley Incident[]

In Mary Geoise, Figarland Garling met with the Five Elders to reveal a preposterous discovery he had made on God Valley and suggested this island as the site of the next Native Hunting Competition. A year later, a God Valley resident named Magnolia showed the townspeople the twin sons she had borne from Garling: Shanks and Shamrock.[33]

On Hachinosu, the Rocks Pirates were devastated to discover that Shakky had been kidnapped, with the assailant managing to kill Marlon to get to her. The World Government moved in on God Valley the next year, and Garling reunited with Magnolia and met his sons for the first time. He told Magnolia that this island would cease to exist and he would soon have a new wife and then stabbed her with his sword. The Marines moved to subdue the townspeople for the competition, and the young sailor Monkey D. Dragon was at a loss as to why they were doing this but was told not to question his orders. Another Marine ship brought slaves, including Ivankov, Ginny, and Kuma, aboard the island. However, the young journalist Morgans managed to get intel that Shakky was being held by the World Government on God Valley, and the news spread to both the Rocks and Roger Pirates, causing them to set course for the island.

The Rocks Pirates were confronted at sea by Harald and his men, and after a brief exchange of words, Rocks asked to speak with the king in private. There, Rocks revealed to Harald that his birth name was Davy D. Xebec; he was secretly part of the Davy Family and a descendant of the legendary pirate Davy D. Jones. God Valley was his homeland, and he had sent his wife and two-year-old son there to be protected by his kinsmen. Shakky's involvement in this affair was a stroke of fate, but unbeknownst to all but Harald now, Rocks only desired to go to God Valley to save his wife and son. He asked Harald for aid, and Harald refused but told his men not to attack the crew as Rocks attacked his ship in anger. On God Valley, the competition was set to begin as Shakky was presented as the grand prize; additionally, it was announced that the Davy Family had been discovered here for the first time in 800 years. Despite the family's secrecy, the World Nobles were well aware of them and so jeered, comparing them to buccaneers and saying they needed to be wiped out. As they sat in a cage waiting to be released and hunted, Rocks' wife Eris comforted their son Teach.

Dragon entered a storeroom to find a mortally wounded Magnolia with Shamrock and Shanks, and she asked him to take her sons off the island and away from Garling.[35] Dragon accepted her plea and ran off with the twins as she passed; the natives and slaves were then released onto the island before the World Nobles were given the go-ahead to hunt them down. As the Celestial Dragons commenced their slaughter, Dragon was left horrified and could not help but tranquilize one of them, saving Ivankov's life. Right then, the Rocks and Roger Pirates arrived and made landfall, and the Knights of God mobilized to protect the other World Nobles as Garp arrived to protect his troops.[36] As Dragon tried to find an escape ship, Satchels Maffey found him with the twins and fired a laser into Dragon's back, allowing her to seize Shamrock. Maffey prepared to finish off Dragon but was then cut through by Newgate, and Dragon realized she had overlooked Shanks, who had landed on the ground nearby. The Marines moved to evacuate the World Nobles and Shakky before the pirates converged, but Sommers discovered that the former Kuja had subdued every soldier trying to reach her. Sommers overpowered Shakky and decided to kill her as the pirates came, and Roger told Rayleigh to go and rescue her as Newgate held him up. Rayleigh entered the room and struck down Sommers with a single blow before Shakky tearfully embraced him.[37]

With Shakky taken, the Rocks Pirates turned their focus to treasure while Rocks did battle with Garling. Having found Eris and Teach, Rocks carried them away as Garling and his companion pursued them, having turned members of Rocks' family into demons. Rocks had managed to deal Garling some wounds, which were slow to regenerate, but knew he could not let the Knights overtake him. However, Eris told her husband to put her down and let her escape with Teach on her own, promising to reunite with him at Lulusia. Rocks acquiesced and turned around to confront Garling. Meanwhile, Charlotte Linlin had taken the Uo Uo no Mi, Model: Seiryu from Ivankov and plotted to give it to Perospero, only for Kaidou to hit it out of her hand and consume it. As the pirates battled over the treasure and the Marines fired on citizens who tried to escape, Saturn conversed with Imu about the potential outcomes of this chaos. Saying that nothing new should come of this, Imu personally intervened in the battle by possessing Saturn, drawing the attention of Rocks, Roger, and Garp. Having beaten Garling, Rocks turned to face Imu and was joined by Newgate, Kaidou, and Linlin.[38]

Imu's shadowy form rose up from Saturn's gyuki transformation and began using it to wantonly attack anyone in the vicinity, including Marines. Rocks addressed Imu directly, saying he had come to fulfill Davy Jones' promise. Roger, Garp, Newgate, Linlin, and Kaidou soon joined Rocks in attacking Imu, with the six fighters unleashing devastating simultaneous attacks on the ruler, but Imu was unaffected by their barrage. While this was happening, John used his Jiki Jiki no Mi ability to attract all the treasure to him, only to be stopped by Ganzui using the Bomu Bomu no Mi. Dragon and Shanks got caught in the explosion, causing them to be separated as Shanks crawled into a treasure chest. Imu rose up even higher and spotted Eris and Teach running away. Rocks jumped up to slash Imu but was then impaled by two tendrils as Imu performed Domi Reversi on him, telling the pirate to slaughter his wife and child himself.[39] Rocks managed to avoid transforming at first, as he taunted Imu over the ruler's fear of the Davy name. Imu retorted by saying the Davy bloodline would be extinguished here and revealed that the World Government would soon subjugate the giants of Elbaph as soldiers. This enraged Rocks, but he could do nothing as his demon transformation took effect.

The demonized Rocks swiftly overpowered Kaidou and Linlin, and his clash with Newgate caused one of God Valley's landmark mountains to collapse. As everyone else rushed to escape the island, Rocks caught up to Eris and Teach and pursued them to the coast, but Kuma came in and pushed them to safety right as the location was engulfed by Marine cannon fire. Thinking Eris and Teach dead, Imu ordered Rocks to slaughter everyone remaining on God Valley. Roger and Garp rose to confront Rocks in the midst of his carnage, and as they moved to fight, they suddenly heard a voice coming from Rocks pleading for them to kill him.[40] Their clash split the heavens, and Roger and Garp decided to team up to handle Rocks. While the duo unleashed their most powerful attacks on the demon to try and find a way to break his curse, Rocks' crew went their separate ways as Newgate was approached by a pirate named Polo Gram, who proposed teaming up with him. With Imu's work done, Saturn departed God Valley and declared that the island never existed. At the climax of their battle, Roger and Garp caught their breath in rubble as they tried to figure out a way to break through Rocks' Haki defenses. Roger proposed hitting the pirate with a combined critical mass of their Supreme King Haki, and the duo recognized the pain Rocks was going through as they unleashed their combined final blow. Though much of this incident and the Rocks Pirates' exploits would be hidden by the World Government, the effects of Rocks' downfall impacted the world regardless.[41]

Roger, Garp, and Rocks lay incapacitated in the rubble as Rocks began to regain his senses. Seeing Garling, Sommers, and Maffey approaching him, Rocks silently hoped the other two would escape and thanked them for stopping him as Garling finished him off with his sword. Rayleigh and Gaban rescued Roger and Garp, and their parties successfully set sail from God Valley before it completely collapsed into the sea. A number of new stories began on this day, and once Roger recovered, he and his crew surveyed the treasure they had taken but were then shocked to find Shanks in one of the chests. Garp was enraged to learn that the newspaper gave him all the credit for Rocks' downfall, and Sengoku had to hold him down. Later, Garp visited Dragon, who was being held in a prison cell. Though Dragon said he was quitting the Marines and expressed disgust toward his father, Garp agreed with him and gave Dragon the key to his cell so he could escape. On Elbaph, Loki wept openly upon hearing the news. He recalled one of his interactions with Rocks, in which he stated his belief that the pirate was Nika. Rocks told Loki that Harald held the key to making him the King of the World but said Harald would not team up with him because of love.[42]

Regicide and Patricide: The Truth of That Day[]

In the aftermath of Rocks' downfall, Harald attacked a marauding giant in a fit of rage. Expressing regret for not being there for his friend in his time of need, Harald saw no other option but to double down on his path toward peace and so could not stand to see any more senseless violence by giantkind. He told Ida and Jarul that he would now fully dedicate himself to pursuing his goal and asked Hajrudin and Loki to work together to support Elbaph after he was gone. Harald then sailed to Marineford and ripped off his horns in a show of prostration, offering to do anything the World Government wanted, even become a slave, in order to atone for the sins of the giants.[42]

About 33 years ago, the Grand Line was abuzz over tales of a giant who rose out of the mists to annihilate pirates, which in truth was Harald assisting the World Government. Nine years later, the Great Pirate Era began, and Harald was given the chance to visit Mary Geoise for the first time. The Five Elders invoked a Shallows Covenant to make him a Devoted Blade of God, saying he could one day achieve full knighthood and gain affiliation with Elbaph. Four years later, Harald visited Neptune on Fish-Man Island and spoke of the troubles with his son Loki. Neptune said it was prophesied that he would have a daughter in four years and jokingly suggested she be betrothed to Loki. Harald offered a toast to the future of Loki and Neptune's "shaggy" daughter. That same year, Saul came to Elbaph, and Harald commissioned him to open a school there. Five years later, Fisher Tiger raided Mary Geoise and was secretly assisted by Shanks, who had recently returned to his family and become a Devoted Blade. Not long afterward, Harald returned to the Holy Land and met Shanks, who acted snobbish and claimed to have hated his life in the Lower World.

In Fisherman Village, Ida fell ill. Loki hunted down a bull to feed her, saying he needed the bar open again, but despite his coarse words, one of Ida's attendants knew this was a demonstration of his love for her. Loki went to Brewers Village, where Estrid was from, and learned that her relatives had poisoned Ida to prevent her from becoming queen. This sent Loki into a rage, and he burned down the village while declaring he held nothing but contempt for Estrid and that Ida was the only mother he had.[43] Loki was imprisoned for this act, and Harald visited Ida at her bedside before she died. Despite Saul and Ida's reservations about his efforts to undo all of Elbaph's past, Harald expressed confidence in the path forward and expressed his gratitude to Ida for what she taught him. Ida died at the end of their conversation, and Harald, Hajrudin, and Loki all mourned her.

A year later, the Five Elders offered Harald a promotion to knighthood after Shanks suddenly deserted Mary Geoise. While heading to the Empty Throne, Harald revealed that he planned to abdicate his throne while serving as a Knight, but Imu replied that this was for him to decide as Harald came face-to-face with the secret ruler for the first time. Imu bestowed the Depths Covenant upon Harald, making him an undying and powerful Knight of God. As his final task before Elbaph joined the World Government, Harald was told to create an Abyss on Elbaph. Once he did this, however, Imu telepathically commanded him to mobilize the citizens of Elbaph into an army serving the World Government. Harald was shocked by this request, as he had pursued affiliation so his kingdom could live in peace, but Imu said that the king could not disobey this order under the Covenant. A panicked Harald ordered his guards to chain him to a pillar and summon Loki and Jarul.[44] However, he then briefly lost his mind and slew one of his guards, causing him to order that they kill him, resulting in the scene Loki and Jarul first encountered upon their arrival.

During his visit with Gaban, Shanks learned that Roger had had a son and revealed to Gaban about the Shallows Covenant he and Harald possessed. Following their first meeting, Shanks later spoke to Harald outside of the Holy Land and revealed his true allegiance as a pirate. Knowing what the Depths Covenant would entail, Shanks wished to warn Harald about it before it was too late, but he and Gaban then noticed a burst of Haki coming from the castle. Loki and Jarul managed to pin down Harald, and the king revealed to them that the World Government had corrupted him; he instructed the duo to have Loki eat Elbaph's treasured Devil Fruit. As Loki started running toward it, Harald lost his mind again and slew more guards before impaling Jarul's helmet with his sword as he intended to acquire the Devil Fruit first. However, Shanks and Gaban then arrived and held up Harald with their attacks, allowing Loki to reach the treasure room.[45] To his shock, the hammer Ragnir began attacking him on its own, and Harald told him that no one had managed to acquire the fruit before because of the hammer's protection. Gaban gave Harald longer-lasting wounds using Supreme King Haki, revealing the Roger Pirates had regularly fought off attempts by the Knights of God to get Shanks back; however, Harald was able to counter with his own Haki. Unable to get around Ragnir, Loki met it head-on with his own Haki-imbued hammer, and the force of their clash shattered Loki's hammer. Ragnir bounced around on the ground before transforming into a beaten-up squirrel, having consumed the Risu Risu no Mi, Model: Ratatoskr. Having been bested by Loki, Ragnir took him to the treasure chest and allowed him to eat the fruit.

Loki transformed into a massive creature and used his arms to inflict powerful blows on his father. Finally, Harald came to his senses again and prepared for his son to strike him down, saying it was the only way to stop him from complete corruption. He also thanked Loki for showing his care for Ida by burning down the village that murdered her. As he spat curses at his father for forcing him to commit this act, Loki swung Ragnir into his father's chest, dealing a Haki-infused blow so great that it bypassed Harald's immortality and killed him.[46] While Loki sat weeping at his father's corpse, Shanks, Gaban, Jarul, and the surviving guards walked out of the castle. Recalling his father's complete disdain for the outside world, Shanks broke down in anger and sadness over the pain his family and the World Government had inflicted on people like Harald. In Mary Geoise, Imu was shown trembling as the Elders reported on Harald's death. Imu refused to send more Knights to investigate, saying the Covenant invoked a cost on his part as well. Imu remarked that Harald was an anomaly among the giants, and their inability to bring him to heel was a reminder that the World Government was incompatible with Elbaph, which Imu called ""D." Despite Harald wishing for Loki to tell Elbaph what really happened in the castle and assume the throne, Loki could not bear to try convincing the populace. He chose instead to go out to sea and avenge his father, even though this meant the other giants would surely hold him responsible for Harald's murder.[47]

The Calm Before the Storm: Nidhöggr Rises[]

In the present day, Gaban confirmed Loki's account to Hajrudin, who understood that the World Government was actually responsible for Harald's murder. Hajrudin unlocked Loki's final chain, apologizing for doubting his love for their father and asking him to help fight for Elbaph. Right then, a Draugr MMA fell into the Underworld, and Loki volunteered to handle it while the New Giant Warrior Pirates, Zoro, Sanji, Chopper, and Gaban went up to the Sun World. With Luffy accompanying him, Loki jumped toward the Draugr and swung Ragnir, encasing the MMA in ice with a single strike called Niflheim.[47] As they rode up the lift, Gaban explained to the others who the attacking Knights were and what powers they possessed. They reached the Sun World to find it in pandemonium from the MMA and demon Giant Warrior Pirates, and Kashii explained to the group what was going on. Road, Gerd, and Goldberg rushed to help save the hostage children, Sanji headed to the library to help Nami's group, and Zoro accompanied Hajrudin and Stansen to confront the demons. Though Brogy's group was bearing down on him, Jarul refused to evacuate the Western Village as he instead broadcasted his voice to Elbaph. He revealed the true circumstances of Harald's murder and that Loki was in fact innocent. With the present-day attack being the World Government's latest attempt to subjugate the giants, Jarul also revealed the truth of the impending Great War and proclaimed that Elbaph would oppose the World Government when the day came for the world to split in two.[48]

Zoro's group confronted the demons at the Western Village, and to avoid being transformed himself, Zoro jumped above the giants and used their heads as launch points before unleashing a whirlwind cutting attack with Three Sword Style. Dorry and Brogy then moved to attack Zoro, and he barely managed to get out of the way, resulting in the demon captains striking each other instead. These attacks caused them to briefly regain their senses, and the duo quickly agreed to die to break their curse as they proceeded to decapitate each other. However, their bodies were then flipped on the ground, restoring their normal selves alive and well. With the knowledge that death would break the Demon Covenant, Dorry and Brogy joined Zoro, Hajrudin, and Stansen in confronting the remaining demons, and did not hold back in an effort to inflict fatal wounds.[49] Meanwhile, Kashii tried to protect Chopper and Gaban from D. D. Tee and Rhodes, but could not bring himself to harm his crewmates. With no other option, Chopper entered Monster Point and swiped at one of the demons, and to the shock of everyone this move caused him to revert back to normal.[50]

Meanwhile, Brook moved down the branch route to check on Gunko, who he said looked remarkably similar to a princess named Shuri whom he knew in his living years. Brook said that Shuri had killed his benefactor and would certainly be much older in the present day, but could not shake off the seeming connection. This caused Gunko's eyes to grow wide, and she released Brook's bindings while telling him to run. Imu was displeased to find Gunko's memories returning, and reasserted control over her mind and body before attacking Brook. Gunko's moment of hesitation allowed Nami, Usopp, and Jinbe to get free as well, while Sommers guided the children down the last remaining stretch to the ship. A Nika MMA then approached, and was attacked by the children's relatives as they closed in. Gerd, Road, and Goldberg then launched a combined attack on the MMA, causing it to crash into and destroy the ship and sea clouds below it.[51] Sommers panicked as Gunko's arrow would cause the children to walk off the dock to their deaths, but he could not reach her while she was possessed. However, as the children's relatives unsuccessfully attempted to stop the arrow, Sommers decided to instead take pleasure in their tragic deaths. The children began weeping as they regretted their past bad behavior, and with no other recourse, their relatives embraced them so that they may fall to their deaths together, starting with Ripley embracing Colon. However, the group's fall was quickly broken as they landed on an inflated Luffy, who was being carried atop Loki who had transformed into the legendary beast Nidhöggr.[52]

Loki flew up to the Sun World and deposited the children at the Western Village, not caring about the danger there. Sommers moved to retake the children and kill their parents, but Zoro cut apart the thorns he launched. Luffy, in his Gear 5 form, then grabbed a lightning bolt and blew Sommers apart with Gomu Gomu no Dawn Thor Rifle, freeing the children from the thorns. Upon seeing Loki's transformed state, Gaban recalled his conversation with Jarul on the day of Harald's death. Jarul revealed that Elbaph's national treasure was the Ryu Ryu no Mi, Model: Nidhöggr, and its power would be maximized if consumed by a person with ancient giant heritage. This power was previously held by the legendary Warrior God, who was said to have battled Nika alongside his companion Ratatoskr. Following the Warrior God's death, legend held that Ratatoskr's spirit began inhabiting his weapon Ragnir and guarded his Devil Fruit in the centuries since until choosing Loki to have it. Loki chased away the remaining MMA before pulverizing them with a lightning blast from his mouth, and through Gunko, Imu was able to lay eyes on Nidhöggr once again.[53]

Sanji reached Nami's group and used Diable Jambe to burn Robin's thorns off. Nami brought out Zeus to rain on the library and put out the fire, but Killingham summoned a Lindworm MMA to eat Zeus. However, Franky, Lilith, and Bonney reached the school, and Lilith procured Omni-Drain Converters from the Vegapunk clone's box which she said could draw energy from their surroundings. By activating them, they flew around the library and froze it, taking the flames and converting them into energy. Lilith then gave Franky a new type of cola, called Super Vega Cola, which she said would drastically increase his cyborg body's capabilities. Killingham sent the Lindworm to eat the library, but a combined attack from a high-powered Franky, Jinbe's Fish-Man Karate, and Sanji's Ifrit Jambe blew apart the MMA.[49] The Straw Hats then tore apart Killingham's body with relative ease and held his parts to prevent them from regenerating together. Nami turned her attention toward the other fires that had been started, and when Luffy and Loki arrived, she reprimanded them for Loki's reckless attacks starting the fires. As this happened, Usopp went to find Brook and was attacked by Imu. Despite being overwhelmed, Usopp refused to run away, saying it would be humiliating to do so in the place he always dreamed of visiting. Moreover, Usopp was enraged at the World Government for attacking Elbaph in an underhanded way, and he told Imu to have the decency to face the giants head-on as the ruler attacked him and Brook through Gunko. Usopp tried to fire an exploding skull, but Imu redirected it back at him. However, Luffy and Loki then arrived to confront Imu, who referred to them as Nika and Nidhöggr.[50]

Luffy took Usopp and Brook to Nami's group while Loki readied to fight Imu, who realized that he had been behind mysterious attacks on government entities at sea several years ago. When Luffy rejoined the fight, Imu's shadowy form emerged from Gunko's body and attempted to use Domi Reversi on Luffy and Loki, only for the technique to have no effect. Luffy then started punching Imu before Loki came jumping in; reverting to his giant form, Loki and Ragnir attacked Imu with Niflheim, encasing Gunko's body in a block of ice and causing Imu's form to dissolve. Luffy then ran out of energy and exited Gear 5. Across Elbaph, most of the enemies had been vanquished and the people turned their attention to putting out the fires Loki started. Zoro's group still had some demons to fight, but Chopper then arrived and revealed his strange ability to undo the curse. Robin and Saul entered the Owl Library, and were shocked to find all of the books disappeared along with Biblo.[54] They eventually found a secret tunnel around the back, and discovered that Biblo had somehow managed to transport all of the books there.[55] Gerd discovered Sommers attempting to reform his body and told Hajrudin and Zoro, showing them the Knight's exposed heart which was encased in steel. Sanji left Killingham's parts tied to a tree, while Luffy and Loki went into the Walrus School to eat and regain their strength.[56]

Gods and Devils: The King of the World Descends[]

On Mary Geoise, Imu informed the Five Elders that he would be personally departing the Holy Land for a time.[54] The older four Elders were shocked by this news and tried to dissuade their boss from doing this, but Garling said they could not stop Imu. Imu dismissed the Elders' suggested alternative courses of action, saying that everything leading up to this had been preordained and could not be prevented. On Elbaph, several large masses of black matter erupted around Aurust Castle as an Abyss circle was projected in the sky, and the nearby houses started coming to life as Saint Nerona Imu, the King of the World and one of the First Twenty, appeared. After briefly coughing up blood, Imu used the power of the Akuma no Mi to transform into a devil and move to the rooftop of the castle.[56] Asking who was the king of this land, Imu instantaneously moved to the village and sliced off Gerd's fingers in the blink of an eye to free Sommers. Hajrudin moved to attack Imu, but was kicked aside by Stansen as Imu severed Stansen's leg. Zoro attacked Imu with King of Hell Three Sword Style, but Imu easily countered him with his devil tail before summoning a black flame from his hand called Omen, which he launched toward Zoro with the speed and force of bullets. Hajrudin moved in on Imu again, and he launched a larger Omen at the giant, engulfing him in a massive explosion. Imu then summoned a ring of Omen around Sommers to give him a power boost, then flew toward Killingham, firing an explosive Omen at the frozen Gunko along the way. Imu gave Killingham an Omen before being attacked by Sanji, but he swiftly overpowered the cook with a kick. Imu was then confronted by Loki, who was just the person the King of the World was looking for.[57]

Loki attacked Imu using Ragnir, but Imu blocked the strike using Omen, which he then used to increase his size and generate weapons to strike the giant. Imu said his power was what gave mankind the domination they craved, but Loki refused to submit himself to receive it. Imu briefly got angry while thinking of Joy Boy during this exchange, causing his defenses to slip as Loki hit him into the Adam Tree with Ragnir. Loki then transformed into Nidhöggr and fired a lightning blast at Imu, but Imu dissipated it and cracked a smile upon seeing Nidhöggr again with his own eyes, though referred to the figure as "traitor".[58] Imu was also pleased to see Ragnir again, and the squirrel-hammer summoned snow to aid in Loki's lightning creation, resulting in a large-scale clash of lightning and Omen. Imu said if Loki and Luffy would not serve him, then he would eliminate them like he did with Douzan.[59] Meanwhile, Lilith and Bonney tended to Sanji, but despite his grievous wounds the cook began moving to find Killingham, who had gone to kidnap the children again. Sommers procured a new ship and brought it to port with his bare hands, telling Killingham to bring the hostages in the next five minutes. Chopper tended to the wounded in the village, managing to stitch Gerd's fingers back together without issue, while Goldberg took Zoro away at his request. However, Killingham faltered in his mission by accidentally summoning what he himself most feared, that being the Rain God Zaza; a woman made of water was now flooding the village and seizing the children.[55] The children's relatives moved to protect them once again, ignoring Chopper's warnings that they would die from their previous injuries if they did so.[59]

Despite Imu's Omen strike, Gunko's body remained encased in ice, and Usopp and Nami asked Brook why he had gone after her. Brook admitted that Gunko strongly reminded him of the princess of his homeland, whom he lost contact with long ago when she traveled to Mary Geoise. Though she would be 77 years old now, Brook could not help but believe that she and Gunko may indeed be one in the same after witnessing her briefly come to her senses and call out to him, and wondered if she might have someone controlling her. 70 years ago, in the now-fallen Esperia Kingdom, Brook served as the captain of King Reuven's Battle Convoy and was close with his young daughter, Princess Shuri. The kingdom came under attack by members of the Moulon Family, who were pursuing Queen Candelle. Reuven pleaded for Brook to stop them, but the musician revealed he had already cut them when they came to the port, with their wounds only opening as they reached the opera house.[59]

Seven years later, Brook told Shuri about his debt of gratitude toward her father and mother. At age 11, an orphaned and homeless Brook met Reuven at the trash dump and offered him some food, causing the two to become friends. One day, a group of Marines mistook Brook for a drug mule and punished him under the belief he was attempting to give them chili powder instead of the drugs they were covertly buying. Reuven then intervened with the Battle Convoy to save his friend, and despite the potential consequences, he and convoy captain Candelle attacked the Marines on behalf of Brook. Brook was then taken to Esperia Palace, where Reuven revealed that he was a prince. He gave his friend lodging and instruments at the palace, as well as an education.[60] Brook also learned from Candelle as her page, and during that time, Celestial Dragons ended up coming ashore on Esperia. Candelle ended up bedridden for months after this event, but afterwards things turned better as Reuven ascended the throne, married Candelle, and they had Shuri. Brook and Shuri made many memories together, but in her fifteenth year, Esperia was flooded with smog which took away its music and killed Candelle. To make things worse, Esperia still had to pay the Heavenly Tribute but could not afford it. With the alternative being to send a thousand slaves, Reuven elected to go to war against the World Government. Brook prepared to lead the Battle Convoy in this conflict, and played music for the civilians to encourage them. However, the World Government sent overwhelming numbers against Esperia, setting it ablaze as Brook had to fight just to survive. Upon hearing that the government had breached the palace, Brook headed there only to find Shuri and Reuven transformed into demons, with the princess having slain her father.[61] Brook rushed to help Reuven, but was surprised to hear Shuri say that she possesses World Noble heritage, that Reuven was not her real father, and that the government's demand for slaves was a lie by Reuven to not surrender her. Despite Brook's remarks, Shuri angrily said that Reuven led his own people to death and that killing him meant ending the war. The man beside Shuri revealed himself as Manmayer Gurou, her biological father, and ordered her to take down Brook. Brook refused to fight as Shuri impaled him in the head and left the palace for Mary Geoise. Brook survived and was treated by his associates from the Battle Convoy, but the news about Reuven's death, Esperia's collapse, and Shuri's betrayal greatly overwhelmed him.

Returning to the present, Brook realized that Shuri was victimized by the government's control and decided to put his trust in her again, urging the other Straw Hats to help him rescue her frozen body. Meanwhile, Loki continued in his battle against Imu, as Imu mentioned the giants were defeated 800 years ago protecting their children. Before Loki could attack again, an energized Luffy appeared behind him and punched Imu, demanding he leaves Elbaph.[62] Imu countered by launching black flames at them, and using Stigma to pierce Loki's chest and knock him down towards the Straw Hats. This also made Ragnir hit the block of ice surrounding Gunko, causing it to melt. Before Imu prepared to attack Luffy, Luffy tossed aside his jacket to divert the attack, and subsequently entered his Gear 5 transformation. Meanwhile, Zoro confronted Sommers at the Western Village port in order to thwart their plan to abduct the giant children. At the village, Sanji began fighting against Killingham to make the Zaza MMA disappear and protect the kids. Killingham boasted that he could not be defeated without Sanji using Supreme King Haki, which made Sanji remember Gaban telling him that he had the potential to awaken it, but he himself must become like a king that can help Luffy become the Pirate King.[63]
